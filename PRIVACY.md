# Privacy Policy — Dhamma Echo

**Effective Date:** November 26, 2026

Dhamma Echo is built with a strict privacy-first, local-first design:

- **No Accounts:** Dhamma Echo does not require registration, login, or any personal profile.
- **No Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal information, usage analytics, crash telemetry, or device identifiers.
- **No Ads or Trackers:** The app contains no advertising SDKs or third-party tracking libraries.
- **Local Storage:** Bookmarks, playback progress, reading positions, preferences, and downloaded audio/video/PDF files are stored locally on your device only.
- **Network Requests:** Network connections are made only when you stream or download Dhamma teachings from the catalogue sources.
