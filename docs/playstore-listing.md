```text
Dhamma Echo is a calm, local-first library for discovering, listening to, watching, and reading 47,960 authentic Dhamma teachings across 256 teachers and 1,315 collections—with zero accounts, zero ads, and zero tracking.

EXPLORE THE ARCHIVE
• 30,563 Audio Talks (MP3, plus browser fallback for legacy WMA)
• 14,865 Video Discourses (MP4, plus browser fallback for legacy WMV)
• 2,532 eBooks (PDF, plus browser fallback for legacy DOC)
• 1,194 Abhidhamma teachings and curated teacher collections

BUILT FOR CALM, FOCUSED STUDY
• Bilingual English & Myanmar (မြန်မာ) interface with script-aware typography
• Light (Raw Cotton) and Dark (Warm Obsidian) themes
• Background audio playback with lock-screen and notification controls, ±15s skip, 0.75×–2.0× playback speed, queue drawer, sleep timer, and automatic resume
• Fullscreen landscape & portrait video player with speed and resume support
• Built-in PDF reader with Light, Sepia, and Dark reading tints, page scrubber, and reading-position memory

OFFLINE-FIRST & PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Instant offline SQLite catalogue search across titles, teachers, and collections
• Single-item and bulk series downloads with optional Wi-Fi-only protection
• Bookmarks, history, and downloads stored strictly on your device
• No sign-in, no analytics, no telemetry, no ads, and open source (AGPLv3)
```
